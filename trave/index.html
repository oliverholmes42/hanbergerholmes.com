Trave

Your game backlog, finally organised.

Trave is a simple library for the games you play, own and want to play. Track what you're playing, sort your backlog, and rank everything you finish in an S–F tier list.

What it does

  • Playing, Backlog, Beaten and Abandoned sections, with a Paused marker
  • Backlog buckets: Next up, Interested and Maybe
  • Mark which games you own and filter your backlog to what you can start now
  • Rank finished games in an S–F tier list
  • Pick one favourite game for your profile
