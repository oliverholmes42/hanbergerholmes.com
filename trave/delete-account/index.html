Delete your Trave account

Last updated: 30 October 2026

You can delete your Trave account and all data linked to it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete it in the app

  1. Open Trave and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm in the warning dialog.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, for example if you have uninstalled it, email support@hanbergerholmes.com with the subject "Delete my Trave account". Send the message from the email address registered on your account so it can be verified. Your account will be deleted within 30 days, usually much sooner, and you will get a confirmation reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email address and password hash.
  • Your whole library: games, statuses, ownership marks, platform tags, tiers, and your favourite game.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you is kept after deletion. Encrypted backups containing your data are overwritten as they rotate, within 30 days. Basic server logs without library content may be retained for a short period for security purposes.

For more detail on how data is handled, see the Privacy Policy.
