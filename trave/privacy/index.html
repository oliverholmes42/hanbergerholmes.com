Trave – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Trave is a mobile app for tracking the video games you play, own and want to play. This policy explains what personal data the app handles, why, and what rights you have. Trave is built and operated by one person and is not run by a company.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Oliver Hanberger Holmes, Sweden.
Contact: support@hanbergerholmes.com

2. What data Trave collects

Trave collects only what it needs to run your account and show you your library:

  • Account data: your email address and a password. The password is never stored in readable form; it is stored as a salted hash by the authentication service.
  • Library data you create: the games you add, their status (playing, backlog bucket, beaten, abandoned, paused), whether you mark them as owned, an optional platform tag, your tier rankings, and your favourite game.
  • Technical data: standard server logs and authentication session information needed to keep you signed in and to protect the service from abuse.

Trave does not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or payment information. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

3. Why and on what legal basis

  • To create and operate your account and sync your library across your devices. Legal basis: performance of the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To send emails that are necessary for your account, such as sign-up confirmation and password reset. Legal basis: the same.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Trave does not send marketing email and does not sell or share your data for advertising.

4. Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase hosts the database and authentication. Your data is stored in a data centre within the European Union.
  • An email delivery provider sends account emails (confirmation and password reset). It receives your email address for that purpose only.
  • IGDB (a Twitch / Amazon service) supplies game information such as titles, covers and release data. When you search for a game, the search text is sent to IGDB through Trave's own server. Your email address and account details are not sent.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where that happens it is covered by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long data is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and library data are permanently deleted straight away. Copies in encrypted backups are removed as backups are rotated, within 30 days. See how to delete your account.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data held about you,
  • have incorrect data corrected,
  • have your data erased,
  • receive a copy of your data in a portable format,
  • restrict or object to certain processing.

You can delete your account yourself at any time in the app. For any other request, email support@hanbergerholmes.com from the address registered on your account. You also have the right to complain to the Swedish supervisory authority, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), or to the authority in your own country.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access to each user's library is restricted to that user's own account. No system is perfectly secure, but reasonable technical measures are used to protect your data.

8. Children

Trave is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not intended for them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact the address above and it will be deleted.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top will be updated and, where appropriate, you will be told in the app.

10. Contact
